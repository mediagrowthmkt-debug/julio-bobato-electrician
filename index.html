Yes. Julio Bobato Electrician is fully licensed and insured in Massachusetts. We give you our license number up front, before you even ask.

Yes. We pull the required permits so the work is inspected and to code. That keeps your home safe and your homeowner's insurance valid.

We serve Danvers, Peabody, Beverly, Salem, Middleton, Topsfield and the surrounding North Shore of Massachusetts.
